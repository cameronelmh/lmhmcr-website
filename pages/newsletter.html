MT26 Week 7

Week of 23 November 2026

Hi all,

It's been a long few weeks! Apologies for the delay in getting this mailing list set up, but we're hopefully able to get it working now. In the mean time, to make sure that there isn't anyone that's missing out on any crucial information, we've set up a log of the weekly newsletter ( on the website). This will be updated every week with the latest newsletter, and will also contain a log of all past newsletters.

Again, can only apologies for the delay in getting this working, but we've been working hard to get it working as best as possible!

This week's events

Hope you're all keeping warm as the term winds on! Here's what we've got lined up for you this week – there's something for everyone, so do come along and say hello.

  • Wednesday 25 November, 8:00AM - LMH Tennis Courts: Barry's Bootcamp
    • Our regular Wednesday morning workout is back! A free session that's equal parts exercise, laughter, and community spirit – a brilliant way to kickstart your day, whatever your fitness level.
  • Thursday 26 November, 7:00PM - MCR: Hustings & Merch Drop
    • Nominations for MCR committee positions are now open (closing tonight!), with hustings taking place on Thursday! Do come along! Being on the committee is a great way of getting involved in college life and representing the graduate community. You also get the chance to attend exclusive meetings, formal dinners, and celebrations hosted by LMH.
    • For those on the committee, there's also 5 on-site accommodation rooms reserved for returning committee members!
    • Check your emails for the template!
    • We'll also be doing a merch drop and giving out the merch ordered this term, so do come along to grab yours
  • Thursday 26 November, 9:00PM - MCR: Post-Hustings Wine Tasting
    • Once hustings have wrapped up, stick around and unwind with us for a cosy wine tasting! We'll have a lovely variety of wines on offer, plenty to go round, and non-alcoholic options too so that there's something for everyone to enjoy.
  • Friday 27 November, 7:00PM - MCR: Wadham/LMH Formal Exchange (LMH Leg)
    • LMH is hosting the Wadham/LMH Formal Exchange this Friday! A lovely chance to welcome some new faces to the MCR and enjoy a formal dinner together.
  • Sunday 29 November, 7PM: Gaudy Dinner
    • Rounding off the week in style, we've also got Gaudy Dinner this Sunday 29 November – always a highlight of the term. If you've managed to get a ticket, you should have received a message or email!
  • Sunday 29 November, 12:00PM - Paul Oster Room, MCR: Welfare Brunch
    • And if a relaxed start to Sunday sounds more your pace, join us for Welfare Brunch at 12pm in the Paul Oster Room in the MCR – good food, good company, and a chance to take a breather before the week ahead.

Best wishes,
The MCR Committee
